✦ Brand identity · Mascot design · In-character copywriting
Senior the Lion — a wedding mascot built from star signs
A couple wanted a custom character for their wedding. The groom was a Leo. The bride was a Taurus. So I co-designed a lion with horns — named SR after their initials, personality traits drawn from both signs. Then I wrote every pre-event guest communication in Senior's voice.

Senior's introduction — written entirely by me

The first message guests received, in character:

"I hope you believe in Christmas Miracles,
Because looking at you, I sure do 😉

Oh sorry, you don't know me, but I know you 🫣
I'm the cutest mascot that you ever knew

I'm not a clown, but I can make you smile
I'm not a hottie, but I have some style

I took S from the bride and the groom gave me an R,
That's how I got named SR.

I'm not ordinary, I am a keeper —
No sir, no ma'am. You can call me Senior 😎"
✦ Serialised storytelling · Wedding countdown · Brand voice
A love story told in verse — one chapter at a time
For another wedding, I wrote a countdown series where each message was a chapter of the couple's real love story — told in rhyme, referencing actual places they'd been and moments they'd shared. Guests weren't getting event reminders. They were following a story.

"Destiny let the Cupid play

Made his heart sway
Moving one step closer to the big day
Their forever is only 20 days away"
15 Days to go
"What happened next?
We're all so keen 🙈
They met at their favourite spot again,
And Oh, what a scene!!
Days to THE DAY are only 15!!"
1 Week to go
"Riding on the sleigh, the reindeers sing,
They next met when the Christmas bells ring
Whenever they're around we hear violins
Only a week until their happily ever after begins :)"
✦ Brand concept · Event naming · Narrative design
The wedding that told their actual story
A couple's love story had been built across visits to different places — each one significant. Instead of standard event names (sangeet, mehendi, reception), I named every wedding function after those real locations. Guests weren't attending events. They were retracing the couple's journey together. The décor, the communications, the sequence — all one narrative arc. That's brand strategy applied to the most personal moment of someone's life.
